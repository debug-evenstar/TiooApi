Google Analytics is an app measurement solution, available at no charge, that provides insight on app usage and user engagement.

Google Analytics collects usage and behavior data for your web app. The SDK logs two primary types of information:

(a)    Events: What is happening in your app, such as user actions, system events, or errors.

(b)    User properties: Attributes you define to describe segments of your user base, such as language preference or geographic location.
